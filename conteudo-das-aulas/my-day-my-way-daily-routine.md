**S, ES, IES (for He, She, It in Present Simple)**

| OCCASION | RULE | EXAMPLE |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Most verbs | add -s | plays, works, drinks |
| Verbs ended in O, S, X, Z, SH, CH | add -es | watches, does, dashes |
| Verbs ended in consonant + Y | Drop Y, add -ies | Studies, Tries, Cries, Flies |
