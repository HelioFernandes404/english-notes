# Visual Analogy for At, In, and On

During review, the user correctly chose all four prepositions in professional-context sentences after recalling the analogy: `at` is a point on a map, `in` is inside a container, and `on` is a surface or platform. The user now distinguishes `I am working in the office` from `Let's meet at the office`; future reviews can use realistic work sentences and retrieval practice instead of reteaching the basic analogy.
