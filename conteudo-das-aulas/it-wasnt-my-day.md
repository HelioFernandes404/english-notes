## A Day To Remember

### **Write 1–2 sentences about yesterday or another day this week or last week.**

So, last week I worked a lot because I’m looking for a promotion at my company. Summarizing: I got a new promotion. At my weekend I relaxed. To see my friends, I played basketball and drafted a project.

## Read Emma’s Story. Underline the actions she describes and answer the questions.

> Two days ago, I **had a really crazy day.**
>
> First, I **woke up late and missed my bus**. I felt stressed because I **was late for work**. Then, I **arrived at work without my phone**. I left it at home. I was worried because **I didn’t call anyone**. I planned it another way.
>
> At lunchtime, I went to a café and tried sushi with a colleague. Suddenly, I saw my favourite singer there! We **were very surprised and took a photo**. After work, I met two friends. We **talked and laughed together**. Finally, when I got home, I found a £20 note in my jacket pocket. I **completely forgot about it**.
>
> In the end, it was a great day.

## PAST SIMPLE SYNTAX

|  | Estrutura | Exemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **+** | Subject + V2 / -ed | Everything was not like I planned. |
| **-** | Subject + didn’t + V | I didn’t have my phone. |
| **?** | Did + subject + V ...? | Did you have a great day in the end? |

I had my phone.

I didn’t have my phone.

Did I have my phone?

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Most verbs | verb + -ed | miss → missed; play → played |
| Verbs ending in -e | verb + -d | arrive → arrived |
| Verbs ending in consonant + -y | verb - ~~y~~ + ied | try → tried; cry → cried |
| Verbs ending in CVC | Double final consonant + -ed | plan → planned; stop → stopped |

CVC - Consonant + Vowel + Consonant
