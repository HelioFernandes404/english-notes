|  | **TRANSPORT** | **LOCALIZATION** | **TIME** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AT | N/A | Very specific location | Schedule (às xxhxx) |
| ON | Public transport vehicle. Vehicles you can mount. | On a surface, physical or digital. | Specific day |
| IN | Private vehicle, normally small | Inside something or somewhere, Less specific location | In how much time |
| BY | How you go from point A to point B | A nearby location | Deadline |

### AT

#### Localization

I am at Lojas Renner.

The office is located at Alphaville.

#### Time

Your English class is at 9am.

The standup meeting is at 11:45am

### ON

#### Transport

I’m on the bus to the airport.

My girlfriend and I are on a cruise.

Hélio is on the plane to São Paulo.

### Localization

Hélio is on his chair.

#### Time

My birthday is on August 25th.

The deploy is scheduled on Thursday.

I will see my family on Christmas.

I will travel on my birthday.

I will complete 3 years anniversary in my current company on December 8th 

### IN

#### Transport

I’m in the Uber at the moment.

The driver is in the truck.

#### Localization

Your cellphone is in your pocket.

There is a problem in the code.

I’m in Lojas Renner, at the fitting room.

#### TIME

I have to finish this task in the next six days.

Christmas is in four months.

After this class, I will see you again in six days.

### BY

#### Transport

After this class I will go to the gym by car.

I go to the bakery by bike.

#### Localization

#### Time

We have to finish the refactoring project by the first week of December.
