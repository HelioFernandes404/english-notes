# Teaching Notes

- Use emojis as visual anchors and illustrations when explaining grammar. Keep them helpful rather than decorative.
- Include a simple analogy in future explanations.
- For lesson reviews, teach directly in the terminal/chat: explain briefly, ask one exercise at a time, and give immediate feedback. Do not create HTML unless explicitly requested.

## Teaching preferences

- Start reviews with a clear visual map of the whole rule before asking exercises.
- Introduce one grammar pattern at a time and avoid switching to special cases too early.
- During exercises, focus on grammar first; correct spelling only when it blocks understanding.
