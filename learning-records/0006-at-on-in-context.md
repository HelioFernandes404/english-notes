# At, On, and In in Context

The user correctly distinguished `at` for a specific point or general location, `on` for a specific day, surface, platform, or public transport, and `in` for being inside a space. During review, the user corrected `I am at the bathroom` to `I am in the bathroom`; future lessons should contrast `at the office` (general location) with `in the office` (inside) and avoid direct translation rules.
